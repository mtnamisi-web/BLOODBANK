How this blood-bank system works

A coordination tool for donors, hospitals, and authorized blood-bank staff.

1. Record inventory

Authorized administrators add collected blood bags, component types, locations, and status. Inventory summaries update for connected visitors.

2. Coordinate requests

Hospital accounts submit the requested group, component, quantity, and urgency. Staff review requests and update their status.

3. Schedule donors

Donors request appointments and review their appointment status. Qualified staff make final eligibility decisions in person.

4. Share live updates

Connected pages receive stock and request updates over a WebSocket connection. A disconnected page attempts to reconnect automatically.

Important operational limitations

  • Stock counts are only as accurate as staff-entered records; verify critical availability directly.
  • Compatibility summaries are informational. Clinical crossmatching and transfusion decisions must be made by qualified professionals.
  • Do not use this sample application as a substitute for an approved clinical, emergency, or regulatory system.
  • Before internet deployment, configure HTTPS, secure secrets, backups, audit trails, monitoring, and institution-approved privacy controls.
For emergencies, use established local emergency and blood-bank communication channels.
